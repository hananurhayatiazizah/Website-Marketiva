## Brand & Style

The design system radiates infectious energy, creative confidence, and accessible expertise tailored for vibrant digital commerce and MSME (UMKM) business growth. Inspired directly by the logo’s chromatic playfulness—featuring modular, bold letterforms paired with a delightful candy-toned camera motif—the visual language breaks free from sterile corporate marketing tropes. Instead, it invites small and medium business founders into a collaborative, celebratory creative playground where high-performing marketing feels joyful, tangible, and exhilaratingly achievable.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Playful Pop** with **Contemporary Clean Interface Design**:
- **Radiant Warm Canvas:** Grounded in soft, warm milk and cloud whites (`#FAFAF8` / `#FFFFFF`) that allow multi-chromatic accents to pop without causing visual chaos or eye fatigue.
- **Chromatic Segmented Accents:** Strategic distribution of electric blue, radiant strawberry red, hot bubblegum pink, fresh botanical green, and golden honey amber. Rather than competing, each color commands distinct functional roles—growth metrics, celebratory badges, category filters, and primary conversion triggers.
- **Tactile Rounded Modernism:** Generous, plush border radiuses (`rounded-lg` through `rounded-full`), smooth pill chips, and organic squircle cards reminiscent of camera buttons and stickers.
- **Approachable High-Conversion Dynamics:** Direct, conversational layout flows, energetic micro-copy badges, and clear, welcoming actions geared toward fast consultations and frictionless WhatsApp onboarding.

## Colors

The color palette directly references the vibrant candy-coated personality of the brand logo, harmonizing a 5-tone spectrum across clear structural and contextual tiers:

- **Primary Electric Blue (`#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`):** The operational backbone. Drives primary actions, system anchors, focused links, and core navigation clarity.
- **Secondary Strawberry Crimson (`#E11D48`):** High-energy focal color. Reserved for hero conversion triggers, headline emphasis marks, limited-slot indicators, and flash announcements.
- **Tertiary Fresh Green (`#10B981` / `#16A34A`):** The engine of growth. Dedicated to revenue ROI statistics, verified partner indicators, campaign completion tags, and immediate WhatsApp messaging gateways.
- **Hot Bubblegum Pink (`#EC4899` / `#F43F5E`):** The creative spark. Accents social media management modules, influencer creative kits, photo/video service features, and playful sticker badges.
- **Cheerful Amber Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`):** The joy amplifier. Represents customer reviews, milestone achievements, star ratings, and celebratory promo tags.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Background Canvas: Cream Light (`#FAF9F6`) and Pure Card White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Dark Contrast Body: Slate Deep (`#0F172A`) for bold headline clarity and Slate Medium (`#334155`) for readable paragraph blocks.
  - Borders: Crisp, ultra-soft outlines (`#E2E8F0`) providing tactile framing without heavy structural visual weight.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the unified typographic voice across display, headers, body, and micro-labels. Its geometric contemporary architecture, open apertures, and cheerful curves match the bubbly warmth of the brand identity while ensuring razor-sharp legibility across digital touchpoints.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in ExtraBold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). Large headings can utilize chromatic highlights—wrapping target keywords in pink, blue, or red spans to create that signature playful agency visual punch.
- **Body Copy:** Set in Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to make agency strategies, package scopes, and marketing metrics easy to digest for growing businesses.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in Bold (`700`) with uppercase or title-cased micro-tracking to mimic clean, tactile stickers and interactive tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of `1240px` to maintain focused visual scanning and energetic rhythm:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` page margins. Modular cards use alternating 4-column (triplets) or 6-column (duos) arrangements with organic, asymmetrical accent offsets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Content reflows into two-column balanced arrangements.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Grids collapse into vertical story-like card stacks with prominent thumb-friendly target heights.
- **Component Padding & Rhythm:** Internal card padding is standardized at `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), while section breaks employ `space-xl` scaled to `4rem`–`5rem` on desktop to create breezy, inviting breathing space between case study showcases, service carousels, and client packages.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an optimistic hybrid of **Tactile Ambient Glows** and **Clean Crisp Outlines**, avoiding heavy, murky enterprise drop-shadows:

- **Surface Baseline:** Neutral background `#FAF9F6` with elevated modules resting on `#FFFFFF`.
- **Resting Card Layer:** Supported by a crisp 1.5px soft border (`#E2E8F0` or `#F1F5F9`) paired with a diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Lift & Playful Float:** On hover, interactive cards elevate with a light `-4px` vertical transform and an expanded, color-tinted ambient glow (e.g., blue-tinted `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)` or pink-tinted `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.14)`).
- **Persistent Floating Controls:** Floating WhatsApp conversion pods and sticky action headers use `0 20px 30px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` to assert clear z-index priority and friendly presence.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary, echoing the smooth curves of the brand camera lens and friendly geometric typography:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to form fields, inline code snippets, and internal sub-elements.
- **Component Standard (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / `16px`):** The primary signature radius for action buttons, interactive modules, service feature blocks, and review cards.
- **Hero & Feature Containers (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Used for pricing spotlight cards, case study media containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Reserved for category filter chips, status badges, sticker tags, avatar rings, and floating action buttons.
